Privacy Policy - Simple Expense Tracker

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Simple Expense Tracker! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information You Provide

  • Email Address: When you sign in with Google, we access your email address for account identification
  • Name: Your display name from your Google account for personalization
  • Feedback Information: Name, email, and comments when you contact us through the feedback form

2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Analytics: App crashes, performance data, and feature usage through Firebase Analytics
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Interaction Data: How you interact with app features (to improve user experience)

3. Expense Data

  • Financial Information: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter
  • Starting Balance: Your initial account balance setting
  • Currency Preferences: Your selected currency symbol and date format settings

4. Authentication Data

  • Google Account Information: Authentication tokens for secure sign-in
  • Session Data: Temporary session information for maintaining login state

How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses

  1. Core App Functionality
    • Store and manage your expense records
    • Provide personalized expense tracking experience
    • Sync data across your devices (if enabled)
  2. Authentication
    • Secure sign-in using your Google account
    • Maintain your login session
  3. Customer Support
    • Respond to your feedback and support requests
    • Improve app features based on user feedback
  4. App Improvement
    • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
    • Fix bugs and improve app performance
    • Develop new features
  5. Analytics
    • Understand how users interact with the app
    • Monitor app performance and stability

Advertising

  • Google Mobile Ads: We may display ads within the free version of the app
  • Ad Personalization: Ads may be personalized based on your interests and app usage
  • Premium Version: Ads are removed for premium users

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • Primary Storage: Your expense data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database
  • Encryption: Sensitive data is protected using device-level security features
  • Control: You have full control over your local data

Cloud Services

  • Firebase Authentication: Used only for secure user authentication
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting
  • No Financial Data Sharing: We do not share your financial information with third parties

Data Security Measures

  • Secure Transmission: All data transmitted uses HTTPS encryption
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel can access user data
  • Regular Security Updates: We maintain security standards and update regularly

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We Share Information With:

  1. Google Services
    • Firebase Authentication (for sign-in)
    • Firebase Analytics (for usage analytics)
    • Firebase Crashlytics (for crash reporting)
    • Google Mobile Ads (for displaying advertisements)
  2. Formspree (Feedback Service)
    • When you submit feedback, your name, email, and comments are sent to our support email
    • No other data is shared with this service

We Do NOT Share:

  • Your expense amounts or financial data with advertisers
  • Personal information with data brokers
  • Your data with third parties for marketing purposes

Your Rights and Choices

Data Control

  • Access: You can access all your expense data within the app
  • Export: Export your data as CSV file from the settings
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data from the app
  • Modify: Edit or update your expense entries at any time

Privacy Choices

  • Opt-out of Analytics: Disable data collection in device settings
  • Ad Preferences: Manage ad personalization in device settings
  • Account Deletion: Delete your account permanently from the app

Children's Privacy

  • Age Requirement: This app is not intended for children under 13 years old
  • No Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Parental Control: Parents should monitor their children's use of this app

Data Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Active Account: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Account: Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Legal Requirements: We may retain certain data as required by law

Local Data Control

  • Your Device: You control data on your device and can delete it anytime
  • Backup: We recommend regularly exporting your data for personal backup

International Data Transfers

  • Server Location: Our servers and cloud services are primarily located in the United States
  • Data Processing: Your data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate
  • Standard Clauses: We use standard data protection clauses for international transfers

Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • Updates: We may update this privacy policy periodically
  • Notification: Significant changes will be communicated through the app
  • Effective Date: The "Last Updated" date shows when changes take effect

Contact Information
